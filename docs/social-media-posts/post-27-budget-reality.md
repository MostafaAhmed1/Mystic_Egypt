## Caption
Here's the thing nobody tells you about budget travel to Egypt. It's not the flight that breaks you. It's the taxi that charges you double because you didn't know the fare. It's the hotel that said it was 50 dollars, then added a service fee and a "guide fee" at checkout. It's the camel ride that looked "optional" in the brochure but somehow became mandatory in person.

Those are the real budget killers. Not Egypt itself.

When I built Mystic Egypt, I built it around one principle: you should know exactly what your trip costs before you commit to it. No line-item surprises. No "extras" that appear three days in. No feeling like you're being hustled by someone who got lost on their way to their own tour.

That's why we're UK-registered. Not because it makes our brochures look good, but because it means someone in London can actually hold us accountable if we fail you. And because we operate locally, our prices are genuine Egyptian prices, not inflated international rates with a UK markup added at the end.

A complete Egypt trip with us runs from under 900 pounds for a tight budget to around 2,200 pounds for something more comfortable. Those numbers include flights, transfers, guides, entrance fees, and accommodation. You get the full breakdown before you pay a single pound.
