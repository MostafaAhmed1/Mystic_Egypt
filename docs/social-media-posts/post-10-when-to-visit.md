## Caption Body

**☀️ March–May (Spring):** Perfect for Cairo, Luxor & Aswan. Warm days, cool evenings, balloon weather. Book early — it's peak season for a reason.

**🔥 June–August (Summer):** Upper Egypt sizzles (40°C+) — but the **Red Sea is glorious**: sea breezes, 29°C water, and low-season prices. Dahab & Sharm shine now.

**🍂 September–November (Autumn):** Arguably the *best* all-round time. Comfortable temps everywhere, underwater visibility at its peak, manageable crowds.

**❄️ December–February (Winter):** Ideal for sightseeing — pyramids and temples in sunny 22°C while Europe freezes. Red Sea stays swimmable. Pack a light jacket for desert nights.
