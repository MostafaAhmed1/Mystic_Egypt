## Caption
I've been to the Maldives. I've been to the Great Barrier Reef. And I'm telling you, the Red Sea is different in a way that's hard to explain until you're there.

It's not about the color, though the color is ridiculous. It's not about the visibility, though you can see 40 meters if the day is calm. It's the feeling of being in a place that has been doing exactly this for millions of years, completely unbothered by anything on land.

The fish in Egypt don't perform for you. They just exist. A barracuda the size of a child drifts past like it owns the place. A sea turtle surfaces beside you and looks at you with an expression that I can only describe as mildly disappointed. A school of barracuda moves in a perfect spiral like a silver tornado. And you're just floating there, breathing, realizing that none of your problems have ever been important.

The diving in Dahab is world-class for a reason. The Blue Hole is famous, but honestly, the house reef is where the magic happens. You can walk in from the sand, go 15 meters down, and find yourself in a coral city that's been building itself for 10,000 years.
